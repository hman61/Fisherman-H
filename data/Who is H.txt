I grew up on Aland Island in the Baltic Sea where our family had fish at least 5 days a week. I think that made me get a pallet that now helps me create fish and seafood dishes where the natural flavors shine, as well knowing what best to pairs those fantastic and healthy flavors. I catch most of the fish I make, which allows me to work with freshest "raw materials" and refine every piece of the fish, it also including home grown Pacific North West vegetables that we grow on our property. One of my signature dishes is a half baked and hot smoked seared Chinook Salmon that is one of the best tasting Salmon verities in the world in my opinion although, I'm capable of making American Shad to taste really good as well. I cook primarily by heart where I visualize and associate flavors when decide what to pair things with. I rarely use receipts when I cook, although I do use receipts for dishes that where the proportions and the sequence is important and I definitely I know that I need to practice some classic restaurant dishes and sauces that will be required to know by heart and it will be nice to add some more stellar recipice to the rotation. I also planning  to brush up on baking , although my wife is a killer baker, so I will have fun learning  from her. 



Signature Dish:
I would cook a half baked, half hot smoked fresh caught Coho or Chinook Salmon or a high quality Steelhead with my mothers mushroom, scallions and dill sauce with some steamed new potatoes and a cucumber salad.
For appetizer I would serve roasted beats and onions with some Matjes Herring ( or Pickled Shad if shad is running)  or Fresh salmon row on a piece of toast.
